How to use this studio
  1. Unlock with your dashboard access key.
  2. Save your Sleipnir URL, key and stream profile.
  3. Connect the receiver agent on the machine receiving your PS5 broadcast. Buttons stay unavailable until it checks in.
  4. Press Test PS5 ingest, then start the PS5 broadcast using your verified receiver route. Watch the processed frame count. This test does not forward video to Sleipnir.
  5. Press Go live through Sleipnir. This sends video to the destinations enabled in Sleipnir. Confirm gameplay and audio on those platforms.
  6. Use Reconnect relay if needed, and Stop receiver processing to stop this agent's FFmpeg process. End the broadcast on your PS5 too.
