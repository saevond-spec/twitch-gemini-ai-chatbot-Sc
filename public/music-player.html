Audio routing

Android + PS5 direct broadcast: this page plays music on the phone, but PS5 does not mix phone or IRC audio into its broadcast. Viewers will not hear the tracks with that setup.

To put music in the broadcast, the player audio must be mixed into the stream by a computer running a stream mixer or by audio-routing hardware. If you have computer access, open this page there and mix its tab audio with the gameplay. Twitch chat can still control the player from Android.

Click “Enable audio” once on the device that runs the player. That browser permission only starts playback there; it does not route the sound to Twitch by itself.

Chat controls

!music dubstep · !music club · !music play · !music pause · !skip · !song

Station changes, play/pause, and skip are broadcaster/mod-only. Tracks advance automatically and the playlist loops.

NCS licenses eligible independent creators to use its music on Twitch when they follow its usage policy; it is not public-domain music. Add Music provided by http://spoti.fi/NCS to the stream description. Read NCS usage policy.
