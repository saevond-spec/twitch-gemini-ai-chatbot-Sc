Audio routing

Android + PS5 direct broadcast: this website does not connect its audio directly to the PS5 stream. Viewers receive PS5 game audio and any microphone audio you choose to include.

Free test using the DualSense mic: on PS5, choose the controller microphone as the input device, make sure it is unmuted, and enable microphone audio in broadcast settings. Place the Android phone speaker near the controller mic while music plays. The mic can carry the music to Twitch as room audio, but quality may vary and it can pick up nearby sounds. Music stays controlled through Twitch chat or this website; no PS5 music app is needed.

For a clean, separate music mix, route this player through a computer stream mixer or audio-routing hardware. The phone webpage by itself cannot be added as an independent audio source to a direct PS5 broadcast.

The player tries to start automatically. If your browser blocks sound, tap the audio button once. After playback starts, tracks shuffle and advance without more taps. This starts audio on that device; it does not route sound to Twitch by itself.

Chat controls
