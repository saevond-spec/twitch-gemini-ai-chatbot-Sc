Free Lightstream setup

  1. Open this page in a Chrome tab on the computer running Lightstream.
  2. In Lightstream choose Add Layer → Screenshare, select this Chrome tab, and tick Share Audio.
  3. Click “Enable audio” once on this page. Keep the audio layer unmuted; you can hide its video layer if you only want the music sound.

If you use OBS instead, add this page as a Browser Source and enable “Control audio via OBS”.

Chat controls

!music dubstep · !music club · !music play · !music pause · !skip · !song

Station changes, play/pause, and skip are broadcaster/mod-only. Tracks advance automatically and the playlist loops.
